# 06 — Non-Goals

HEIMDALL is not attempting to become a universal agent framework during this hackathon.

Excluded from MVP:

- unrestricted general-purpose computer control
- production credential management
- autonomous production deployment
- hidden self-modification
- uncontrolled recursive agent spawning
- replacement of container/sandbox isolation with prompt-based safety
- enterprise IAM replacement
- full SIEM or SOC platform
- training a foundation model
- importing the entire Ghost Atlas estate

The product is strongest when narrow: govern a coding agent's authority, show the decision boundary, capture proof, and make repair/promotion explicit.
